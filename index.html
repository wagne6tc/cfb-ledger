Verify this yourself

Nothing here asks you to take my word for it.

  1. Clone this repository.
  2. Run verify_chain.ps1. It recomputes every hash from the card files on disk. Editing any past card breaks its row and every row after it.
  3. Check chain.csv.ots at opentimestamps.org — it anchors the chain into the Bitcoin blockchain, so the timestamps do not depend on trusting me.
  4. Re-grade it yourself: every result follows from a published card plus a public final score.
